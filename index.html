FOUR SACRED DAYS

The Journey of Chhath

From a purifying river bath to the first light of dawn, each day has its own rhythm of prayer.

1

Fri · 13 Nov

Nahay Khay

नहाय-खाय

Devotees bathe in a river, clean the home and eat one pure, simple meal, commonly rice, chana dal and bottle gourd.

2

Sat · 14 Nov

Kharna

खरना

A fast from dawn to dusk, broken after sunset with sweet kheer and roti shared as prasad. The long fast begins.

3

Sun · 15 Nov

Sandhya Arghya

संध्या अर्घ्य

Families gather at the ghat and offer arghya to the setting sun, with soops of thekua and fruit and folk songs in the air.

4

Mon · 16 Nov

Usha Arghya

उषा अर्घ्य

At dawn, devotees stand in the water and greet the rising sun. The fast is broken and prasad is shared with everyone.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions & Answers

What is Chhath Puja?

An ancient Hindu festival honouring Surya, the Sun god, and Chhathi Maiya. It falls on the sixth day (Shashthi) of the bright fortnight of Kartik, a few days after Diwali. It is celebrated most widely in Bihar, Jharkhand, eastern Uttar Pradesh and the Terai region of Nepal, and by communities around the world.

Why are offerings made at sunset and sunrise?

The rituals thank the Sun for sustaining life. The setting sun is honoured in the evening (Sandhya Arghya) and the rising sun the next morning (Usha Arghya), with devotees standing in the water of a river or pond.

What is the fast like?

Devotees, known as vratis, observe a strict fast. After Kharna, many keep a nirjala fast, with no food or water, for roughly 36 hours until the morning arghya. Cleanliness and simple, carefully prepared food are central to the festival.

Can anyone take part?

Yes. Both women and men observe Chhath, and neighbours often help clean the ghats and share prasad. If you visit a ghat, dress modestly, keep footwear away from the offering area and follow local guidance.

How accurate are the countdowns?

They use approximate times (about 5:00 PM and 6:00 AM) in your device's local time. Sunset and sunrise vary by city, so please confirm exact timings with your local panchang or ghat committee.
